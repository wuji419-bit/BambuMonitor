# BambuMonitor Cinematic Promo Design

## Style Prompt

A restrained, cinematic industrial-tech product film for a serious desktop monitoring tool. Frames should feel precise, dimensional, and editorial: dark cool neutrals, real mechanical printer detail, crisp vector interface typography, controlled mint accents, occasional ice-blue information cues, and a deep ink-green build plate. Motion is confident and fluid, with layered depth, masked reveals, spatial camera moves, and clear visual hierarchy instead of decorative spectacle.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#080C12`
- Panel: `#111923`
- Elevated panel: `#182330`
- Primary text: `#F2F6FA`
- Secondary text: `#9CAFC1`
- Mint accent: `#66E3B4`
- Ice-blue accent: `#72B8F4`
- Warning gold: `#E5B95F`
- Build plate ink green: `#163A32`

## Typography

- Chinese: Source Han Sans SC, Noto Sans SC, or Microsoft YaHei.
- English: Inter, Segoe UI, or an equivalent modern grotesk.
- Use tabular numbers for telemetry, progress, temperatures, and timers.

## Motion

- 0.35-0.7 second entrances with varied cubic or spring easing.
- Use masked reveals, card depth, progress sweeps, spatial push-ins, and controlled scan light.
- Every scene has a designed entrance and transition.
- Keep outgoing scenes visually complete until the transition begins.

## What NOT To Do

- No low-resolution image scaled full-screen.
- No purple build plate.
- No generic neon HUD, gradient text, floating orbs, bokeh, or excessive bloom.
- No screenshot slideshow as the primary motion language.
- No random labels, fictional controls, remote-control claims, or generated UI text.
- No wide black dead space around compact or mini windows.
