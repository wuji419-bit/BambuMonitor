Managing five Bambu Lab printers should not mean jumping between five different windows. BambuMonitor brings device availability, job progress, remaining time, temperatures, layers, and AMS filament into one focused desktop workspace. Printers that need attention rise to the top, while cloud status and local connectivity remain clearly separated, so a reconnect never hides the real print job. Move freely between full, compact, and mini layouts. Resize, drag, pin, and position each view, with every mode remembering its own dimensions. Open the camera wall to watch several printers together, then select any feed for a clear, enlarged view. One settings panel controls startup, opacity, cameras, and notification integrations. Status can stay synchronized through the cloud, while camera access remains direct over your LAN or VPN. BambuMonitor is available for Windows and macOS, and is now open source on GitHub under AGPL version three.
